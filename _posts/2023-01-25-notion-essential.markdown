The Notion Certification Exam is designed to test your knowledge and understanding of the basic features and capabilities of Notion. The exam covers four main areas: relational databases, formula property, commenting, and team collaboration. To pass the Notion Certification Exam, you'll need to have some solid understanding of Notion and it's workflow.

But to be very honest, I feel like if you use most of notion features most of the time in your workflow anyway, you would have little to no problem completing the certificate!

So, earning the Notion Essentials Badge through the Notion Certification Exam is a great way to show your expertise in using Notion.
